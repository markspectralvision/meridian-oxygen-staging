Where the platform comes from

The Meridian range is built on an on-demand oxygen generation platform developed in North Las Vegas, Nevada, and installed in facilities across the United States and Canada since 2013, in a different kind of healthcare facility. That record belongs to the platform, not to Meridian Oxygen, and it comes from a different market. Few oxygen generator manufacturers can point to a fielded base of that age. We can, and we tell you whose it is. We tell you that because you would find it, and because the engineering is worth knowing about.

What transfers is the manufacturing, the monitoring design, the remote alarm architecture, the preventive maintenance program and the people. What does not transfer is any claim. Every statement about a Meridian system is made about a Meridian system, in its own regulatory context, and only when it has been confirmed.

How we work

Three commitments to the committee

We sell the machine, not the company

Capital hospital equipment is bought on specification, in healthcare facilities of every size, and healthcare infrastructure decisions outlast the people who make them. Medical equipment bought on evidence stays bought. You will get a specification sheet, a conformance statement and a sizing worksheet before you get a sales call. Compare us on the product. That is where a new company earns its place.

We disclose limits first

Blender purity specifications, low-flow anaesthesia guidance, plant room siting, electrical load and heat rejection are in the packet. Nothing in a Meridian document waits to be discovered in the design meeting.

We keep it running

The service contract funds the duty cycle, the filter changes, the purity verification and the response time. An idle oxygen plant degrades. Ours run, because the contract says so.
